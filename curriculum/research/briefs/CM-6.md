Conventions: equilibrium at q = 0; M symmetric PD; K symmetric; ansatz q = a cos(ωt − δ); normal coordinates ξ_j.

Topics: expand L about a stable equilibrium: V ≈ V₀ + ½qᵀKq (Hessian; stable iff K PD) and T ≈ ½q̇ᵀMq̇ (from CM-3's structure); M q̈ = −Kq; ansatz ⇒ GENERALIZED EIGENPROBLEM Ka = ω²Ma; THEOREMS proved: (i) ω² real, (ii) ω² ≥ 0 iff K PSD (stability), (iii) eigenvectors M-orthogonal — LA-8's proof with ⟨u,v⟩_M = uᵀMv, shown to be a legitimate inner product, (iv) REDUCTION via Cholesky M = LLᵀ: K̃ = L⁻¹KL⁻ᵀ symmetric with the same ω² (derive; why eigh(K, M) works), complete set of modes even with degeneracy; normalization aᵢᵀMaⱼ = δᵢⱼ; NORMAL COORDINATES q = Aξ decouple L (derive with AᵀMA = I, AᵀKA = Ω²); general solution and initial conditions via M-inner-product projections (derive); zero modes (rigid motions; K PSD); degenerate modes and symmetry (CM-4); WORKED SYSTEMS: (1) two pendulums + spring: modes, beats, beat period derived; (2) two masses, three springs with unequal masses; (3) linear triatomic (CO₂): K, M, zero mode, symmetric/antisymmetric stretches, frequency ratio; (4) double pendulum linearized: ω² = (2 ± √2)g/ℓ for equal masses/lengths; (5) loaded string / N equal masses: tridiagonal K, exact modes sin(jkπ/(N+1)), ω_j = 2ω₀ sin(jπ/2(N+1)) DERIVED from the recurrence, then the CONTINUUM LIMIT to the wave equation and F2's standing waves, dispersion ω(k) linear at long wavelength; (6) three masses on an equilateral triangle of springs: modes by symmetry (translations, rotation, breathing, two degenerate shears); DAMPING/DRIVING: Rayleigh C = αM + βK decouples (derive; why engineers assume it), per-mode frequency response, resonance at each ω_j; modal superposition as the engineering method (FEM paragraph); quantum preview: each mode a quantum oscillator (QM-3), phonons, Debye (SM-5), a field is infinitely many oscillators.
