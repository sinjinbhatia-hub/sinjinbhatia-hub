# Brief: CM-8 — Hamiltonian mechanics: Legendre transform, phase space, Poisson brackets, Liouville, canonical transformations, Hamilton–Jacobi
Folder: curriculum/02-physics/a-classical-mechanics/08-hamiltonian-mechanics/ · ~12 hours · prereq CM-4 · unlocks SM-3, QM-4, QM-9, QC-9
Pair with: Taylor ch. 13; Goldstein ch. 8–10; Landau & Lifshitz §40–52; MIT 8.09 Hamiltonian lectures; Arnold ch. 8 (optional); Susskind *Theoretical Minimum* lectures 8–10.
Conventions: H = Σp_iq̇_i − L; q̇ = ∂H/∂p, ṗ = −∂H/∂q; {F,G} = Σ(∂F/∂q ∂G/∂p − ∂F/∂p ∂G/∂q) so {q,p} = 1; phase space R^{2n}.

§1: Hamilton 1834 rewrote n second-order equations as 2n first-order in phase space: geometry visible, a preserved structure (symplectic form) that Liouville (1838) made the foundation of stat mech, Jacobi's method (1837), and in 1925 the classical shadow of QM (Dirac: Poisson brackets → commutators). Mechanics stops being about forces and becomes about structure. §2: phase space as the space of states; the flow as an incompressible fluid; conserved quantities constant along the flow; {F,G} = "how fast F changes along the flow generated by G"; canonical transformations preserve incompressibility; Legendre transform = describe the curve by slopes instead of points.

Topics: LEGENDRE TRANSFORM geometrically: g(p) = max_v(pv − f(v)), p = f'(v), v = g'(p), involution (derive), tangent-line picture, thermodynamic use (SM-2) one paragraph; H as Legendre transform of L in velocities; DERIVE Hamilton's equations from dH; ∂H/∂t = −∂L/∂t, dH/dt = ∂H/∂t; when H = T + V (CM-4 recap); phase-space variational principle ∫(pq̇ − H)dt varied independently (derive; generalizes to QM path integrals); EXAMPLES: oscillator (ellipses), free particle, central force reduced via p_φ, pendulum (libration, rotation, separatrix energy derived), charged particle H = |p − qA|²/2m + qφ (canonical vs kinetic momentum); FLOW: vector field; LIOUVILLE: divergence-free (derive) ⇒ volume conserved; Liouville equation ∂ρ/∂t + {ρ,H} = 0 (derive); SM-3 link (microcanonical uniform density is stationary) explicit; Poincaré recurrence stated; POISSON BRACKETS: properties (antisymmetry, bilinearity, Leibniz, Jacobi — prove one-DOF or general), fundamental brackets, dF/dt = {F,H} + ∂F/∂t (derive), conserved iff {F,H} = 0, Poisson's theorem via Jacobi (prove), {L_i,L_j} = ε_ijk L_k (derive one), {L²,L_i} = 0; generators: H of time, p of translation, L of rotation ({F,p} = ∂F/∂q); CORRESPONDENCE PRINCIPLE dictionary: {A,B} → [Â,B̂]/iℏ, {q,p} = 1 → [x̂,p̂] = iℏ, dF/dt = {F,H} → Heisenberg's equation — the QM-4 bridge, clear; CANONICAL TRANSFORMATIONS: definition, equivalences (preserve Hamilton's form ⇔ preserve fundamental brackets ⇔ symplectic Jacobian MᵀJM = J; prove bracket ⇔ symplectic for one DOF where it's det M = 1); examples: Q = p, P = −q; oscillator polar q = √(2P/mω) sin Q, p = √(2Pmω) cos Q (verify; H = ωP); GENERATING FUNCTIONS: table of four types, type-2 derived from pdq − Hdt = PdQ − Kdt + dF, identity and point transformations; ACTION-ANGLE for 1-DOF periodic: J = ∮p dq, angle advances uniformly with ν = ∂H/∂J (derive); oscillator J = 2πE/ω; pendulum (elliptic; state); ADIABATIC INVARIANT (state; derive E/ω = const for the oscillator by averaging); Bohr–Sommerfeld J = nh (QM-1 link); HAMILTON–JACOBI: type-2 S(q,P,t) with K = 0 ⇒ H(q,∂S/∂q,t) + ∂S/∂t = 0 (derive); separation S = W(q) − Et; oscillator solved completely by H–J; S as the action along the path (∂S/∂q = p, CM-2); WKB/Schrödinger: ψ = e^{iS/ℏ} gives H–J + O(ℏ) (derive leading order) — the capstone page; SYMPLECTIC INTEGRATORS: RK4 destroys area/energy slowly; symplectic Euler and Störmer–Verlet as exact canonical maps of a nearby Hamiltonian (symplectic Euler = two area-preserving shears); why MD and orbital mechanics use them; integrability and chaos one paragraph (Liouville–Arnold stated, KAM one sentence, double pendulum as counterexample).
