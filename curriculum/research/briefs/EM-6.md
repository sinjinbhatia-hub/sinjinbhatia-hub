# Brief: EM-6 — Electrodynamics: Faraday, Maxwell's equations, energy and momentum, gauge freedom
Folder: curriculum/02-physics/b-electromagnetism/06-electrodynamics-maxwell/ · ~12 hours · prereq EM-5 · unlocks EM-7, EM-8, QM-1 (photons), CM-4 (Noether in fields)
Pair with: Griffiths ch. 7–8; Purcell & Morin ch. 7, 9; Feynman Lectures II ch. 16–18, 27; MIT 8.07 notes lectures 15–19.
Conventions: SI; EMF ℰ; flux Φ = ∫B·da; Faraday ∇×E = −∂B/∂t; Maxwell–Ampère ∇×B = μ₀J + μ₀ε₀∂E/∂t; Poynting S = (1/μ₀)E × B; Lorenz gauge ∇·A + (1/c²)∂V/∂t = 0; the Maxwell stress tensor T_ij with Griffiths' sign.

§1: Faraday (1831) found that changing magnetic flux drives current; Maxwell (1861–65) saw that Ampère's law was inconsistent with charge conservation and added the displacement current, which made the equations predict waves at speed 1/√(μ₀ε₀) = the measured speed of light — "we can scarcely avoid the inference that light consists in the transverse undulations of the same medium"; Hertz (1887) made the waves; Poynting (1884) found where the energy flows; the forcing problem: the static equations are mutually inconsistent once anything moves, and fixing the inconsistency unifies electricity, magnetism, and optics. §2: a changing B makes E curl around it (Faraday); a changing E makes B curl around it (Maxwell); each sustains the other and the pair propagates — the picture of a wave as a self-propelling pair of curls; energy and momentum live in the fields themselves, not just in the charges.

Topics in order: Ohm's law J = σE as a constitutive relation (its microscopic justification in one paragraph; the Drude model), resistance, Joule heating P = I²R (derive from the force law); EMF: definition ∮f·dl, sources of EMF, motional EMF ℰ = −dΦ/dt derived from the Lorentz force for a moving loop (the flux rule), the subtle cases (Faraday's disk; the "paradoxes" and why the flux rule needs care — one page); FARADAY'S LAW: the experimental fact that a changing B produces an E with ∮E·dl = −dΦ/dt, hence ∇×E = −∂B/∂t (derive); Lenz's law as the sign; the induced electric field has curl but (if ρ = 0) no divergence — "a magnetostatics-like problem for E" (the analogy table: ∇×E = −∂B/∂t ↔ ∇×B = μ₀J); worked: the induced E of a solenoid with a ramping current (derive); INDUCTANCE: mutual inductance M₁₂ = M₂₁ (derive via the vector potential — the Neumann formula), self-inductance L, the solenoid's L, the toroid's L; the energy stored W = ½LI² and hence W = (1/2μ₀)∫B² dτ (derive via the work against the back-EMF and then via A·J and the product rule — both routes);
