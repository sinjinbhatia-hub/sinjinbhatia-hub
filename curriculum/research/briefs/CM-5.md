# Brief: CM-5 — Central forces and the two-body problem
Folder: curriculum/02-physics/a-classical-mechanics/05-central-forces/ · ~10 hours · prereq CM-4 · unlocks QM-6, SM (virial), QM-1 (Rutherford context)
Pair with: Taylor ch. 8 and ch. 14 (scattering); Goldstein ch. 3; MIT 8.223 lectures 7–10.
Conventions: μ = m₁m₂/(m₁+m₂); r = r₁ − r₂; ℓ = |L|; u = 1/r; k = Gm₁m₂; eccentricity e.

§1: Kepler 1609/1619 empirical laws; Newton 1687 derives them — the origin of mechanics; the two-body problem is the only interacting problem with a complete closed form, the template for reduction-by-symmetry and for hydrogen. §2: two bodies are one body in an effective potential; angular momentum flattens to a plane then to a line; V_eff's shape tells you circles/ellipses/parabolas/hyperbolas before solving.

Topics: two-body Lagrangian; CM separation and reduced mass (derive); planar motion (prove r·L = 0, L fixed) and Kepler's second law dA/dt = ℓ/2μ; plane polar L, p_φ = ℓ, RADIAL EQUATION μr̈ = −dV/dr + ℓ²/μr³, V_eff = V + ℓ²/2μr² from energy; qualitative orbits from V_eff for inverse-square; circular orbits at the minimum; ORBIT EQUATION via u = 1/r and φ as independent variable: Binet d²u/dφ² + u = −(μ/ℓ²u²)F(1/u) with every chain-rule step; solve for F = −k/r² → r = c/(1 + e cos φ), c = ℓ²/μk; conics by e; DERIVE e = √(1 + 2Eℓ²/μk²); a = c/(1−e²) = −k/2E (period and energy depend only on a; say why remarkable); KEPLER'S THIRD LAW T² = 4π²μa³/k from the area law and ellipse area, with the m₁+m₂ correction; first law as e < 1; ellipse geometry (perihelion, aphelion, b = a√(1−e²)); speeds at perihelion/aphelion; VIS-VIVA v² = (k/μ)(2/r − 1/a) (derive); Hohmann transfer worked (LEO→GEO two Δv's); STABILITY of circular orbits for F = −kr^n: derive n > −3 and the radial oscillation frequency; closed orbits: n = −2 and n = 1 commensurate frequencies (ratio 1 and 2), Bertrand's theorem stated with reference; precession of nearly circular orbits under small perturbations (1/r³ term; GR perihelion shift formula stated); SCATTERING: impact parameter, θ(b), dσ/dΩ = (b/sin θ)|db/dθ| derived from the annulus picture; hyperbolic orbits and deflection for repulsive Coulomb; RUTHERFORD dσ/dΩ = (k/4E)² / sin⁴(θ/2) derived completely; 1909–1911 and why sin⁻⁴ meant a tiny nucleus (QM-1); hard-sphere comparison; LRL points to perihelion with magnitude ∝ e (derive); three-body in two sentences.

Worked examples (≥6): Earth–Sun reduced mass vs Earth mass, a, e = 0.0167, perihelion/aphelion speeds; orbit equation solved with constants fixed by E and ℓ; a comet with E > 0: hyperbola and deflection; Hohmann with numbers; radial oscillation frequency for n = −2 and ratio to orbital; Rutherford: 5 MeV alphas on gold, fraction beyond 90° for a given foil (order of magnitude).
