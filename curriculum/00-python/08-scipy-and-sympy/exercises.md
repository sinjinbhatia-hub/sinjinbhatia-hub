## A. Warm-up (do all; 5–10 minutes each)

**A1.** (SymPy gotchas.) With `x = sp.Symbol("x", positive=True)` and `n = sp.Symbol("n", integer=True)`, predict `sp.sqrt(18)`, `sp.sqrt(18.0)`, `sp.Rational(3, 4) * 0.5`, `sp.S(3) / 4 * 2`, `sp.integrate(1 / x, x)`, `sp.diff(sp.Abs(x), x)`, `sp.solve(x**2 + 1, x)` and `sp.sin(n * sp.pi)`. Then explain why `def kinetic(m, v): return 1/2 * m * v**2` returns a float coefficient for symbolic arguments, and fix it two ways.

**A2.** (Result objects.) Write `require(res)`, which returns `res` if it reports success (`.success`, or `.converged` for `root_scalar`) and otherwise raises `RuntimeError` with the result's message. Predict, for each of the following, whether the routine raises by itself, returns a failure flag, or neither; then run them through `require`: (i) `minimize` of Rosenbrock's function from $(-1.2, 1)$ with `options={"maxiter": 3}`; (ii) `solve_ivp` of $y'=y^2$, $y(0)=1$ on $[0, 2]$; (iii) `root_scalar` of $\cos x-x$ on $[0, 1]$ with `method="brentq", maxiter=3`; (iv) `root_scalar` of the same function on $[1, 2]$.

**A3.** (Substitution order.) Write `to_state(expr, funcs, t)` that replaces each $q_i(t)$ and all its derivatives in `expr` by plain symbols, highest derivative first. Apply it to the energy function (3.4) of $\mathcal L=\tfrac12m\dot q^2-\tfrac12kq^2$, and show what the energy becomes if you substitute $q\to x$ first.

**A4.** (`atol`.) A reactant decays, $c'=-c$, $c(0)=10^{-8}$. Predict the relative error at $t=5$ of `solve_ivp(..., (0, 5), [1e-8], rtol=1e-6)` with the default `atol`, then run it. Choose `atol` by the rule of §3.4 to reach relative error $10^{-5}$, and explain both outcomes with (3.8) in two sentences.

**A5.** (Sparse `*`.) For `A = sparse.diags([1, 2, 1], [-1, 0, 1], shape=(4, 4))` converted to `csr_matrix`, to `csr_array`, and to a dense array, and `v = np.ones(4)`, predict the type and value of `A * (A * v)` in each case. Run, then rewrite the expression so that all three agree.

**A6.** (Argument orders.) Without running: (a) is `integrate.dblquad(lambda x, y: x * y**2, 0, 2, 0, 1)` equal to $2/3$ or $4/3$, and what does the other argument order give? (b) which of `special.ellipk(0.5)` and `special.ellipk(np.sin(np.pi / 4))` is the period factor $K$ for $\theta_0=\pi/2$? (c) the entries of `fft.fftfreq(8, d=0.01)`. Run and correct yourself.

## B. Core (do all; these are the module)

**B1.** (The bead on a rotating hoop.) A bead slides without friction on a hoop of radius $R$ rotating about its vertical diameter at constant $\Omega$; $\theta$ is the angle from the bottom, and $\mathcal L=\tfrac12mR^2(\dot\theta^2+\Omega^2\sin^2\theta)+mgR\cos\theta$. (a) Run stages 1–3 of §3.3 and verify $\ddot\theta=\sin\theta\,(\Omega^2\cos\theta-g/R)$, derived by hand. (b) Find the equilibria with `sp.solve`. (c) For $\Omega^2>g/R$ show with SymPy that the small-oscillation frequency about the nontrivial equilibrium is $\omega_*^2=\Omega^2\sin^2\theta_*$. (d) For $R=0.5$ m, $\Omega=8$ rad/s, release the bead at rest at $\theta_*+0.01$, integrate with `DOP853` at `rtol=1e-11`, measure the period with an event, and compare with $2\pi/\omega_*$. (e) Check that (3.4) is conserved to $10^{-10}$. Is it the kinetic plus potential energy in the lab frame? Test numerically.

**B2.** (Stiffness from a PDE.) The heat equation $u_t=u_{xx}$ on $(0,1)$ with $u=0$ at both ends and $u(x,0)=\sin\pi x$ has the solution $e^{-\pi^2t}\sin\pi x$. With $n=200$ interior points and the matrix $A_n$ of (3.12), $\dot{\mathbf u}=-A_n\mathbf u$. (a) Use Theorem 3.1 for $\lambda_{\max}(A_n)$ and predict the least number of `RK45` steps to reach $t=0.1$ from its stability limit $3.31/\lambda_{\max}$. (b) Integrate with `RK45`, `Radau`, `BDF`, `LSODA` at `rtol=1e-8, atol=1e-10`; tabulate steps, `nfev`, `njev`, `nlu` and the error at $t=0.1$. (c) Give `BDF` the Jacobian `jac=-A` as a sparse matrix. Count the right-hand-side calls with a wrapper and compare with `nfev`: what does `nfev` leave out?

**B3.** (A damped pendulum: events and `dsolve`.) $\ddot\theta+\gamma\dot\theta+(g/L)\sin\theta=0$ with $\gamma=0.4\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$, $L=1$ m, released from rest at $\theta_0=0.05$. (a) `dsolve` the linearized equation with exact rational coefficients and `ics=`, and lambdify the result. (b) Integrate the nonlinear equation at `rtol=1e-11` and report the largest difference from the closed form on $[0,10]$; predict its order of magnitude from the neglected term $\theta^3/6$. (c) Collect the maxima $(t_k,\theta_k)$ with an event on $\omega=0$, fit $\ln\theta_k$ against $t_k$ and compare the slope with $-\gamma/2$; compare the spacing $t_{k+1}-t_k$ with $2\pi/\omega_d$, $\omega_d=\sqrt{g/L-\gamma^2/4}$.

**B4.** (Covariance by hand.) Simulate pendulum periods from (3.6) at $\theta_0=0.1$, ten lengths from 0.2 to 2.0 m, noise 0.01 s, `default_rng(0)`. Fit $T=aL^b$ (true $a=2\pi/\sqrt g$, $b=\tfrac12$) with `curve_fit(..., sigma=..., absolute_sigma=True)`. (a) Rebuild `pcov` from (3.10) using the Jacobian of `least_squares` on the weighted residuals; agree to $10^{-5}$. (b) Report the correlation of $a$ and $b$ and explain its sign. (c) Is $b=\tfrac12$ within $2\sigma$? (d) Propagate to $g=4\pi^2/a^2$ by the delta method and compare with Build 5's one-parameter fit. (e) Repeat with `absolute_sigma=False` and explain the ratio of uncertainties with $\chi^2_{\min}$.

**B5.** (QM-3 preview.) $H=-\tfrac12\,d^2/dx^2+\tfrac12x^2$ on $[-10,10]$ with zero ends has eigenvalues near $n+\tfrac12$. (a) Build $H$ with `sparse.diags` for $n=100,200,400,800,1600$ interior points and get the five lowest eigenvalues with `eigsh(H, k=5, sigma=0, which="LM")`. **B6.** (FFT and leakage.) Sample $x(t)=\sin(2\pi\,50t)+0.5\sin(2\pi\,120.3t)$ at 1000 Hz for 500 samples, add Gaussian noise of standard deviation 0.2, and use `rfft` and `signal.find_peaks`. (a) Report the two peak frequencies and amplitudes $2|F[k]|/N$. Why is one amplitude right and the other low? (b) For the noiseless 120.3 Hz tone alone, compare the peak amplitude and the leakage at 200 Hz with and without a Hann window (`signal.windows.hann(N, sym=False)`, normalized by its sum). **B7.** (Extend `project.py`: dissipation.) Change `pendulum_pipeline` so the damping $-\gamma\dot\theta$ enters as a generalized force $Q=-\gamma mL^2\dot\theta$ on the right of (3.3). ## C. Challenge (do at least one)

**C1.** (Resonance curve from events.) For $\ddot\theta+0.5\,\dot\theta+g\sin\theta=F\cos\Omega t$ ($L=1$ m, start at rest), sweep $\Omega$ from 2 to 4 rad/s in steps of 0.05 and record the steady-state amplitude as the mean of the last six maxima found by an event on $\omega=0$ (integrate to $t=60$, `DOP853`, `rtol=1e-9`). Compare each curve with the linear amplitude $F/\sqrt{(g-\Omega^2)^2+\gamma^2\Omega^2}$; report each peak position and height, check that the maxima have settled (their standard deviation), and say which way the peak bends and why.

**C2.** (Boundary-value problems and gradients.) (a) Solve $u''+e^{u}=0$, $u(0)=u(1)=0$ with `solve_bvp` (`tol=1e-8`) from two initial guesses and find both solutions (report $u(\tfrac12)$).
