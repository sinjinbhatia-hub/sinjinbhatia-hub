## Findings (one per item; severity H/M/L)

### A. Math

1. **[M] notes.md §1, "Composite quantum systems" paragraph.** Claims entanglement is forced by "the dimension count $\dim V\cdot\dim W > \dim V + \dim W$". False for the module's main case: two qubits give $4 = 2+2$. The correct count is that product states $\mathbf v\otimes\mathbf w$ fill a surface of dimension $\dim V + \dim W - 1$ (scaling $\mathbf v$ up and $\mathbf w$ down gives the same tensor) inside the $\dim V\cdot\dim W$-dimensional space; for two qubits $3 < 4$. Fix: state the $n+m-1$ count.
2. **[M] notes.md §3.11, "What just happened" after Theorem 3.14.** "$\dim(V\times W) = \dim(V\oplus W) = n+m$. For two qubits, $4 > 2+2$; for ten qubits, $1024 \gg 20$." $4 > 4$ is false, and the comparison with $\dim(V\oplus W)$ is the wrong one (the simple tensors are the image of $V\times W$ under $\otimes$, a surface of dimension $n+m-1$, not a subspace). Fix: compare $nm$ with $n+m-1$: $4 > 3$ for two qubits, $1024 \gg 63$ for two five-qubit registers. Add an assert in project.py: the Jacobian of $(\mathbf v,\mathbf w)\mapsto\mathbf v\otimes\mathbf w$ has rank $n+m-1$.
3. **[L] notes.md §2, "A vector in $V\otimes W$ is a matrix".** "So the SVD of $C$ should tell you how entangled $\psi$ is. That is Theorem 3.15, and the number of nonzero singular values is the Schmidt rank." Theorem 3.15 is the rank-1 criterion; the SVD/Schmidt-rank statement is Theorem 3.16 (as the header line also says). Fix: cite Theorems 3.15–3.16.
4. **[L] notes.md §3.15, "Contraction" paragraph.** "For the $(1,1)$ tensor $A^i{}_jv^j$ the contraction is $A\mathbf v$." $A^i{}_jv^j$ is already contracted (a $(1,0)$ tensor); the object being contracted is the $(2,1)$ tensor $A^i{}_jv^k$. Fix wording.
5. **[L] notes.md §3.5, proof of Theorem 3.8 (second statement).** Invokes $\operatorname{rank}A = \operatorname{rank}A^\top$ (LA-3) for the dimension count, but the count already follows from rank–nullity for $T^*$, the first statement and Theorem 3.7 (and in fact re-proves $\operatorname{rank}A = \operatorname{rank}A^\top$). Not wrong; hypothesis not needed. Fix: give the two-line count and note the payoff.
6. **[L] notes.md Appendix A.4.** "every rotation has an axis: $\det(R-I)=0$ because $R$ has eigenvalue 1, as LA-7 shows via $\det R = 1$ and the eigenvalues coming in conjugate pairs of modulus 1". LA-7 proves conjugate pairs (Thm 3.16) and $|\lambda| = 1$ for 2D rotations only; the 3D axis argument is not in LA-7. Fix: make the three-line argument self-contained, citing LA-7 only for conjugate pairs.
7. **[L] solutions.md A2(b).** "Forgetting the conjugate gives ... $\langle\mathbf w,\mathbf u\rangle = (1-i)u^1 + 2i\,u^2$, which is $\bar\varphi$, not $\varphi$." That functional is not $\overline{\varphi(\mathbf u)}$ (which would also conjugate $u^1,u^2$); it is the functional with conjugated coefficients. Fix wording.
8. **[M] solutions.md C2(a).** Parameter count: "4 total, minus the global phase already counted ... $13 - 3 = 10$". The 13 never had a global phase removed; by the same bookkeeping used for the two-qubit sanity check in the same paragraph ($4+4+1-2-1 = 6$), the three-qubit family has at most $13 - 4 - 1 = 8$ parameters, not 10. Conclusion ($< 14$) unchanged. Fix the arithmetic.
